**The LFO, the step sequencer, and the panel in pages.** The LFO moves the pitch, the filter or both, at FAST (about 1 Hz to 5 kHz) or
SLOW (0.05 to 18 Hz) rates, or plays half a cycle as each note starts (1 SHOT), with saw, triangle, square or
sample-and-hold waves. FAST and 1 SHOT restart with each note.

The panel now works in pages: a button shows its page, pressed again its next, and K1–K4 edit what's on the
screen; the top right shows which page, such as 1/2.

The drums have a page of their own (ARP), where the F♯–G♯–A♯ black keys play them and glow, and each drum's decay
can be set, as the hardware decay mods allow. The MIDI channels are settable, the keys' lights can be set (GLOBAL
2/3), and the monotribe's LFO controllers work (CC 1, 16, 81–83).

The step sequencer (SEQ): 8 steps for SYNTH, BD, SN and HH, each step with a half-step slot; per-part active
steps (polyrhythms), gate times, 16-step synth, swing, tempo 10–600 BPM, MIDI clock in and out. On SEQ the white
keys are the part's slots and the black keys pick the part; SEQ STEPS sets the active steps and clears; SEQ EDIT
writes notes into steps. REC then PLAY records the keys and the drum pads. The pattern is saved with SAVE.

Bluetooth MIDI devices are now connected from GLOBAL's DEVICES page (K1 picks, SAVE connects), and REC is the
sequencer's. Settings saved by earlier versions don't carry over.
