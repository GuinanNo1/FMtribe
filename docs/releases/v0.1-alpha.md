FMtribe 0.1-alpha: the first build, for testing the platform.

**This is not the instrument yet.** It plays fm1-base's simple test voice (sine or triangle) under the FMtribe name, to check installing, Bluetooth MIDI, USB audio and the screen mirror on real FM-1s. The synth, drums and sequencer come in later versions.

- Install from the browser: https://guinanno1.github.io/FMtribe/install/ (Chrome or Edge, FM-1 on USB).
- Try it first in the emulator: https://guinanno1.github.io/FMtribe/emu/
- To go back, the installer's last section puts M-VAVE's firmware back.

Install at your own risk. FMtribe is independent and not affiliated with M-VAVE or Korg.
