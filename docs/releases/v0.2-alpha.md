**The synth voice and the drums.** One oscillator (saw, triangle or square) and noise into a two-pole MS-style
low-pass filter that self-oscillates, a VCA with the monotribe's three fixed envelopes (Decay, Gate, Attack), and a
bass drum, snare and hi-hat. All synthesised, written from scratch from the published filter papers and from
analysis of public recordings of real units; the figures are estimates until compared side by side with one.

Panel for now: K1–K4 CUTOFF, PEAK, NOISE, LEVEL; SELECT RHYTHM; PRESET the wave; ALGORITHM the envelope;
EDIT, GLO, HOME play BD, SN, HH. OCT− / OCT+ in six steps. MIDI: channel 1 notes and pitch bend, channel 10 drums
on 36, 40, 42, CC 80 (envelope) and CC 7 (level).
