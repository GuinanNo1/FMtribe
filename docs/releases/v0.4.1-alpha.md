**Fixes after 0.4-alpha.**

- The header shows the page you're on (VCF VCA, STEP, MIDI...) where FMTRIBE was, so the labels aren't crowded.
- On the SEQ pages, PRESET picks the part: SYNTH, BD, SN or HH. The black part keys on STEP still work too.
- EDIT edits the picked part. For a drum it shows its 16 slots, each step and its half, and K4 ON sets each hit;
  SELECT moves through the half-steps too.
- HOME is the start page: LEVEL (the synth), RHYTHM (the drums), CUTOFF and PEAK. HOME 2/2 has RANGE and MOTION.
- RANGE works: NARROW glides fast between notes played legato, WIDE glides slowly, KEY jumps. It stands in for the
  monotribe's continuous ribbon. It's on the VCO page too.
- MOTION VOLUME (Ver.2): while the pattern plays, hold ACTIVE STEP (D♯4) and touch the white keys, left silent and
  right full; with REC on, each synth step keeps its level. Turning LEVEL while recording records it too. MOTION on
  HOME 2/2 turns the recorded levels off and on.
- GATE TIME HOLD now works as Korg's does: with it on, recording new notes keeps the gate times already recorded.
- DRUM PREVIEW (PREVIEW, GLOBAL 2/3): on, the part keys play their drum as they pick it; off, they pick silently.
- Settings saved by 0.4-alpha don't carry over.
