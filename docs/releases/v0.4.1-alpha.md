**Fixes after 0.4-alpha.**

- The header shows the page you're on (VCF VCA, STEP, MIDI...) where FMTRIBE was, so the labels aren't crowded.
- On the SEQ pages, PRESET picks the part: SYNTH, BD, SN or HH. The black part keys on STEP still work too.
- EDIT edits the picked part. For a drum it shows its 16 slots, each step and its half, and K4 ON sets each hit;
  SELECT moves through the half-steps too.
