**The panel, rearranged after the monotribe's.** The pages now follow the monotribe's panel from left to right, and
STEP puts the monotribe's row of step buttons on the FM-1's keys, with their lights as the step LEDs.

- **The same on every page:** OCT− and OCT+ set the octave, and SELECT the VCO's wave. On the SEQ pages, SELECT moves
  the step cursor instead. On LFO, ALGO sets the LFO's wave.
- **STEP:** the first eight white keys are steps 1–8 (all sixteen with 16-STEP SYNTH). Their lights show the part:
  dark for a step that doesn't play, dim for an empty active step, half-lit for a step with a note or hit, lit for
  the step playing. F♯3, G♯3, A♯3 and C♯4 pick BD, SN, HH and SYNTH; hold a drum's key and press a step for a
  half-step hit. Hold D♯4 (ACTIVE STEP) and press steps to switch them on or off for playback.
- **GATE TIME (F♯4):** while it plays, hold it and touch the white keys to set the gate, left short and right long,
  as the monotribe's ribbon does; with REC on, each step keeps the gate it plays with. Stopped, it's the Ver.2
  multi-function key: INITIALIZE ALL, SYNTH, RHYTHM and ACTIVE STEP on steps 1–4, SAMPLE & HOLD, 16-STEP SYNTH,
  GATE TIME HOLD and HALF TEMPO on 5–8.
- **EDIT:** the synth's steps as a piano roll; move with SELECT, set NOTE, GATE and ON with K2–K4, or play notes in.
- **The manual** has a new section, "Setting gate times", and new screenshots.
- RANGE (WIDE, NARROW, KEY) is on the VCO page; the keys play KEY until the ribbon modes arrive.
- Settings saved by 0.3-alpha don't carry over.
