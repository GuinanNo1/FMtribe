**Drum rolls, and the step row spread out.**

- **Drum rolls (Ver.2).** Press FX, or the rightmost black key (F♯5), to turn ROLL on; then a DRUMS pad rolls its
  drum while held, from 8ths to 128ths (ALGO on DRUMS sets the rate). On STEP, with a drum picked and the pattern
  playing, hold GATE TIME (F♯4) and touch the white keys to roll it, 8ths at the left and 128ths at the right.
  With REC on, rolls are recorded into the steps and play back.
- **The step row, spread out.** An 8-step part's steps are every other white key (F3, A3, C4, E4, G4, B4, D5, F5),
  with a blank key between; the 16-step synth uses all 16. The multi-function key's steps follow.
- Control keys glow on STEP and DRUMS, F♯5 included; the free black keys stay dark.
- Settings saved by 0.4.3-alpha don't carry over.
